Mimicry

1Record the target voice

The voice the other person will hear. Only use a voice that is yours or whose owner has agreed. 30–60 seconds of clean speech works best.

Read aloud, naturally: “The old lighthouse keeper counted six bright ships crossing the harbour before sunrise. ‘Please bring fresh bread, warm tea and a jar of honey,’ she said, smiling. Thick fog rolled over the quiet water, yet the lamp kept turning, steady and kind. By noon the sky was clear, and children ran along the shore, laughing, chasing gulls, and shouting questions about faraway places. Was it twenty-three or thirty-two? Nobody could quite remember.”

2Record your own voice

Used so the converter knows what “you” sound like. Read the same text in your normal speaking voice (20–30 s).

3Create the voice profile

Samples are cleaned automatically (rumble removed, noise reduced, silence trimmed, level normalised).

4Try it before going live

Record ~8 seconds of yourself and hear how it comes out. Wear headphones.

5Go live

Your voice goes in, the target voice comes out of a virtual microphone for any call app.
